Post once. Reel Reposter does the rest.

Reel Reposter is a free tool for short-form creators. When you publish a reel on Instagram, it publishes the same video to your TikTok account and your YouTube channel as a Short, with your caption. It can also publish your older reels, a few per day.

How it works

  1. 1. Connect your accounts. You sign in to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube with their official login pages and choose what Reel Reposter may do.
  2. 2. Choose your settings once. Who can see each post on TikTok, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, and how many older reels to send per day.
  3. 3. Post on Instagram as usual. Within minutes your new reel is on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Every post is listed in your own log, so you always know what was published and where.

Who runs it

Reel Reposter is built by Bla Matban, a Moroccan creator education team, for its own channels and for the creators it teaches. It runs inside each creator's own Google account; we never see your videos or passwords.
