Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What Reel Reposter accesses

  • Instagram: your reels (video file, caption, link, date) through the official Instagram API, so they can be published elsewhere.
  • TikTok: your basic profile (name, avatar) to show which account is connected, and permission to publish videos you choose to send.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to your channel.

Where your data goes

Reel Reposter runs inside your own Google account (Google Apps Script). Access tokens are stored in that script's private settings. A log of what was published is kept in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. Videos are downloaded from Instagram only to be uploaded to TikTok and YouTube, and are not stored afterwards.

We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising. We do not run our own servers and do not receive copies of your videos, captions, tokens or passwords.

Google user data

Reel Reposter's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads use the YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can disconnect at any time: remove Reel Reposter from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions), your TikTok account (Settings > Security > Apps and services) and your Instagram account (Settings > Apps and websites). Deleting the script and its log sheet removes all data it kept.
